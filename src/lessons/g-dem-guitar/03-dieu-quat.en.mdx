Each rhythm below is a **common, simplified version** for practice — in real life every player has their own variation. Practise each rhythm in this order:

1. **Listen** at the original tempo to learn the "tune" of the strumming.
2. **Lower the tempo** to 60 – 70% and play over **one chord**.
3. Play over the whole progression, **muting Guitar** to accompany the drums and bass yourself.
4. Switch on **speed up gradually** to reach full speed.

## Ballad / Pop

The most basic strumming pattern. The mantra: "**down – down up – up down up**".

<RhythmPlayer rhythm="ballad" progression="C G Am F" />

## Slow rock (12/8)

Each beat split in three, with mutes on beats 2 and 4. Perfect for slow, emotional songs.

<RhythmPlayer rhythm="slowrock" progression="C Am F G" />

Many chord books and scores write slow rock in **6/8**. The feel is exactly the same — every beat split in three — only the bars are cut differently: one 12/8 bar equals two 6/8 bars. Count "**1** 2 3 **4** 5 6", mute on 4. Songs written in 6/8 often hold each chord for two bars, hence "C*2" below.

<RhythmPlayer rhythm="slowrock68" progression="C*2 Am*2 F*2 G*2" />

## Disco

Mutes on beats 2 and 4 with continuous eighth-note strumming — happy and lively.

<RhythmPlayer rhythm="disco" progression="Am Dm G C" />

## Waltz (3/4)

Three beats: **bass – chop – chop**. Remember to change the bass note with each chord!

<RhythmPlayer rhythm="valse" progression="C G G C" />

## Bolero

Bass on beats 1 and 3, mutes on beats 2 and 4, with upstrokes in between. Very common in Vietnamese ballads.

<RhythmPlayer rhythm="bolero" progression="Am Dm E Am" />

## Blues / shuffle

The swaying "long – short" feel. Down on the beat, up on the last triplet of the beat.

<RhythmPlayer rhythm="shuffle" progression="A7 D7 A7 E7" />

## Plenty more rhythms

The **Strumming** page (in the navigation bar) has Slow, Rock, Foxtrot, Boston, Cha-cha, Rumba, Tango, Surf and Reggae too — and lets you **create your own rhythms**.

| Rhythm | Time | Character |
|---|---|---|
| Slow | 4/4 | Slow, with a mute on beat 3 |
| Rock | 4/4 | Steady, driving downstrokes |
| Foxtrot | 4/4 | Bass – mute – alternate bass – mute |
| Boston | 3/4 | A slow waltz with added upstrokes |
| Cha-cha | 4/4 | Three "cha – cha – cha" strums at the end of the bar |
| Rumba | 4/4 | Syncopated 3 – 3 – 2 accents |
| Tango | 4/4 | Crisp, strong mutes |
| Surf | 4/4 | Fast, continuous down-up strumming |
| Reggae | 4/4 | Only short chops on beats 2 and 4 |
