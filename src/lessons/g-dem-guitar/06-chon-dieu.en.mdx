You now have a whole wardrobe of rhythms. The final question: **which rhythm fits this song?**

## Three questions when listening to a song

1. **Groups of 3 or 4?** Tap your foot and count: if "1 2 3 – 1 2 3" feels natural → 3/4 (waltz, Boston). If "1 2 3 4" → 4/4.
2. **Is each beat split in two or in three?** If you hear an even "1 & 2 &" → straight time (ballad, pop, disco…). If you hear "1 trip-let" or "long – short" → split in three (slow rock, blues).
3. **Fast or slow? Happy or sad?** Slow and emotional → ballad, slow, slow rock, bolero, or picking. Fast and happy → disco, rock, surf, cha-cha.

| The song is… | Try |
|---|---|
| Slow and emotional, in 4 | Ballad, slow, ballad picking |
| Slow, each beat split in three | Slow rock (strummed or picked) |
| In 3, swaying | Waltz, Boston |
| Lyrical, moderate 4/4 | Bolero |
| Happy, danceable | Disco, cha-cha, rumba |
| Powerful, energetic | Rock, surf |
| Bluesy | Shuffle |

<Callout type="tip">
There is no single "right" rhythm. A song can be accompanied with several rhythms — players choose a rhythm to express their own interpretation. When unsure, start with **ballad** (in 4) or **waltz** (in 3), then experiment.
</Callout>
